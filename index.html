Frequently Asked Questions

What products does Bell Matches manufacture?
We manufacture resale matches, advertising matchboxes, special matchboxes, custom label boxes, toothpicks and notebooks. Our customers include wholesalers, retailers, hotels, restaurants and international buyers.
Do you offer private label manufacturing?
Yes. We offer complete private label and OEM manufacturing, covering your branding, box design and packaging. You share the idea, and we produce it to your standards.
Can I place bulk wholesale orders?
Absolutely. Our high capacity production lines support bulk orders at competitive prices with flexible quantities. As a custom matchbox wholesale supplier, we can also tailor designs to your order.
Are your products quality tested?
Yes. Every batch goes through raw material checks, strike safety testing and packaging verification before dispatch, so you receive only what meets our standard.
What are your packaging and product specifications?
We make wooden safety matches only, with match heads manufactured to EN 1783 standards. Carton size, quantity per carton and gross/net weight can be customised to your order, and custom printed matchbox packaging is available on request.
What is the shelf life of your matches?
Stored in a dry place away from moisture, our safety matches have a shelf life of 4–5 years.
