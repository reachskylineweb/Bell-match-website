Social Compliance & Ethical Sourcing

At Bell Match Company, we follow ethical and sustainable manufacturing practices supported by recognised third-party certifications. This gives our export partners, wholesalers and distributors confidence in our commitment to ethical, safe, and responsible production worldwide.

ISO 9001:2015

Quality Management System

Our facility is ISO 9001:2015 certified (Certificate No. 176509-2015-AQ-IND-RvA), issued by DNV Business Assurance, Netherlands. This confirms that our quality management system meets international standards.

FSC® Certified

Responsible Forestry Standards

We are FSC® Chain of Custody certified (License Code FSC-C101804), issued by Preferred by Nature. This confirms that the wood used in our matches is sourced responsibly and can be traced through the supply chain.

amfori Member & ICS

Registered amfori member

We make use of amfori BSCI. amfori BSCI (Business Social Compliance Initiative) is a comprehensive programme designed to identify, assess, and mitigate social risks within supply chains. amfori BSCI enables us to exercise ESG due diligence, enhance transparency, and address significant violations of working conditions within international labour rights frameworks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bell Match Company socially and ethically compliant?
Yes. Bell Match Company is a registered member of amfori, and our facility is audited under the amfori BSCI Code of Conduct and ICS. These audits assess fair labour practices, safe working conditions and responsible manufacturing. We also hold ISO 9001:2015 and FSC® Chain of Custody certification. Copies of all certificates and audit reports are available on request.
Is Bell Match Company ISO certified?
Yes. Our facility holds ISO 9001:2015 certification (Certificate No. 176509-2015-AQ-IND-RvA), issued by DNV Business Assurance, Netherlands, confirming that our quality management system meets international standards.
What certifications does Bell Match Company hold for exporting safety matches?
Bell Match Company holds ISO 9001:2015 (quality management), FSC® Chain of Custody certification (responsible wood sourcing), CE marking (EU product safety), and amfori BSCI membership (social compliance due diligence).
Is Bell Match Company suitable for EU/international safety match imports?
Yes. With CE marking for EU market compliance, ISO 9001:2015 quality certification, FSC® responsible sourcing, and amfori BSCI membership, Bell Match Company is positioned for international export compliance requirements.
