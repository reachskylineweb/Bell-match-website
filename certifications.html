Our manufacturing is supported by recognised third party certifications, giving export partners, wholesalers and distributors confidence in how we make our matches.

FSC® Certified

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bell Matches ISO certified?
Yes. Our facility holds ISO 9001:2015 certification for its quality management system, issued by DNV Business Assurance, Netherlands.
Is Bell Matches wood sourcing FSC® certified?
We hold FSC® Chain of Custody certification (License Code FSC-C101804), issued by Preferred by Nature.
Is Bell Matches socially and ethically compliant?
Bell Matches is a registered amfori member and uses amfori BSCI to support fair labour practices, safe working conditions and responsible manufacturing.
What certifications does Bell Matches hold for exporting safety matches?
ISO 9001:2015 (quality management) and FSC® Chain of Custody (responsible wood sourcing), plus amfori membership for social compliance. Copies of certificates are available on request.
