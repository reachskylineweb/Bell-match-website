Our Company Profile

Igniting sparks of inspiration

The Bell Match Company is an admired organization of professionals renowned for exceptional customer service and an impeccable safety and compliance record. Bell Group of companies have interests in engineering, hotels and pyrotechnics. What makes us different is our ability to understand the complete needs of customers and deliver creative solutions. With well-differentiated product offerings and a sustainable manufacturing approach, we offer customers flexibility, stability and absolute peace of mind!

Technology and a motivated workforce are our twin strengths and we take pride in our culture that fosters continual improvement and collaborative problem solving. With a plant spread over 5 acres and the latest innovations and machinery to count, we are a key player in the export segment with a footprint in over 25 countries.

Welcome to a world where service meets commitment, safety meets innovation and success greets you…. Welcome to Bell Matches.
