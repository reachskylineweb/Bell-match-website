Our Company Profile

Trusted Manufacturer & Global Exporter of Premium Wooden Safety Matches

Bell Matches is an established match manufacturer and a trusted safety match manufacturer, built on quality, safety and customer commitment. As part of the Bell Group of Companies, we bring responsible business practices and decades of experience to every order.

Our 5 acre facility, modern machinery and skilled team handle every stage in house, from splint to finished box. This lets us offer flexible, customised solutions as a matchbox manufacturer and exporter serving importers, wholesalers and private label brands worldwide.

Service meets commitment. Safety meets innovation. Partnerships built to last.
